## 1. Amazon rules we follow (2025-2026)

**Main image**
- Pure white background RGB 255,255,255
- Product fills 85% or more of the frame
- No text, logos, badges, watermarks, insets
- Real photo of only what is sold; no props not included
- 1000 px minimum longest side for zoom; we use 2000 x 2000 px
- JPEG preferred; TIFF/PNG ok; max 10,000 px

**Other images**
- Use all 8 secondary slots (PT01-PT08) + 1 video
- Mix: hero infographic, lifestyle, feature close-ups, size/scale, how-to/cleaning, in-the-box, own-brand comparison
- Mobile first: over 55-60% of Amazon visits are on phones; images must read at thumbnail size
- Text on secondary images is allowed; keep it short and true

**Video**
- 15-60 s (aim 30-45 s), 1920 x 1080 recommended (min 1280 x 720), MP4/MOV, 16:9
- Captions if anyone speaks; no URLs, prices, reviews, star ratings or promo overlays
- Listings with video report 30-80% higher conversion (industry estimate)

**A+ Content**
- Basic A+: up to 5 modules per page (Premium A+: up to 7, needs Brand Story on all ASINs + 5 approved A+ in 12 months)
- No prices, discounts, 'sale', shipping or time-limited offers
- No guarantees or warranty promises
- No competitor names or comparisons to other brands; comparison chart = own products only (up to 6)
- No unproven claims ('best', '#1'), no health/medical claims, no seller contact info or links
- Images RGB JPG/PNG, under 2 MB each, 72 dpi min; no animated GIF
- Text limits: four-image module about 300 characters per block; sidebar about 400; our copy stays well under (headline <= 50, body <= 250 characters)

**Brand Story**
- Hero 1464 x 625 px (3000 x 600 px also used in 2026)
- ASIN cards / focus image 362 x 453 px
- Up to 7 cards; links to own ASINs only
- Same no-promo, no-competitor rules as A+
