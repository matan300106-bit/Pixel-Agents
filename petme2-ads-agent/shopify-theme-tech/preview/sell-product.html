Questions

Can I use wet food?

No. PETME2 feeders are made for dry food (kibble).

Can it feed two pets?

Yes, the 3L Dual Bowl and the 5L Elevated feeders split food into two bowls. The 3L Camera Feeder has one bowl.

Do I need WiFi?

WiFi is needed for the app: schedules, portions and alerts. Setup steps are in the manual.

How do I clean it?

Take off the bowls and the food container and wash them. The manual shows which parts are dishwasher safe.
