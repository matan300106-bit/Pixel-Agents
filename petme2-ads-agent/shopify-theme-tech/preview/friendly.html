FAQ

Good questions

Do the feeders work with wet food?

No. PETME2 feeders are made for dry food (kibble).

Which feeder is best for 2 pets?

The 3L Dual Bowl or the 5L Elevated WiFi. Both split food into two stainless steel bowls.

Do I need WiFi?

WiFi is needed for the app: schedules, portions and alerts.

Are the fountains loud?

No. Every PETME2 fountain uses a quiet pump made for use inside the home.

Are the fountains easy to clean?

Yes. Every PETME2 fountain comes apart for cleaning.

Is shipping free?

Yes. Shipping is free on every U.S. order.
