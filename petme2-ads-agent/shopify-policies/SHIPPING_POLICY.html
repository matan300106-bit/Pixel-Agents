Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Free shipping

Shipping is free on every U.S. order. There is no minimum order amount.

Where we ship

We currently ship to U.S. addresses only. We do not ship internationally at this time. If you need to ship to a P.O. box, please contact us before placing your order.

How orders are fulfilled

Orders are fulfilled by Amazon (Multi-Channel Fulfillment) from warehouses in the United States. Your order is shipped in plain, unbranded packaging, so the box may not show the PETME2 name.

Processing and delivery times

  • Orders usually leave the warehouse within 1–2 business days.
  • Most orders arrive in 3–5 business days after they ship.

These are typical times, not guarantees. Delays can happen, for example during holidays or due to carrier issues.

Tracking

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. If you can't find the email, check your spam folder or contact us.

Problems with your delivery

If your package is late, damaged or missing items, contact us with your order number and we will help. See our Refund Policy for returns and our 30-day money-back guarantee.
