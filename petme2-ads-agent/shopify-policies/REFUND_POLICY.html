Refund Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

We want you and your pet to love your PETME2 product. Every order is covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee, counted from the date your order is delivered.

How to start a return

  1. Contact us first, within 30 days of delivery. Use our contact form or email us at matan300106@gmail.com.
  2. Include your order number and a short note about the reason for the return.
  3. We will reply with the return steps. Please do not send items back without contacting us first.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your item arrived damaged, is defective, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 30 days of delivery and include a photo of the problem. In this case we cover the return shipping. For damaged or defective items, we may send a replacement or issue a refund without asking you to return the item.

Change of mind

If you simply changed your mind, contact us within 30 days of delivery. We'll tell you the return steps; return shipping details are shared when you contact us.

Refunds

Once we receive your return, we will issue a refund to your original payment method. Refunds are usually processed within 5–10 business days after we receive the return. Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post the refund to your account.
