## How the budget works
- Daily budget = the most a campaign may spend in a day on average. Amazon may go up to 25% over on a single day (Keyword Test spent $31 on a $25 budget), but not over the month total.
- Money is spent per click. When a campaign hits its budget, its ads stop for the rest of the day (Keyword Test stopped at 4:28 PM, so it missed evening shoppers).
- Budgets add up to $98/day, but real spend is about $55–65/day: only Keyword Test and Competitors use their full budget. The others don't win enough auctions (top of search share under 5%).
- So **budget is not the problem. Bids and keywords decide profit.**

- Prices are still not raised. Each $2 on the dual feeder and $5 on small fountains adds profit to every sale, organic too. At $24.99 the 2L (16% conversion before) becomes profitable with ads again.

## Profit rules from now on (Guardian checks these daily)
- Each campaign: cost per order must stay under the product's profit (stainless $13.86, dual $11.27).
- A keyword with spend ≥ its product's profit and 0 orders gets −20%. A search term at 1.5 × target cost per sale ($17 for stainless) with 0 orders becomes a negative.
- Raise bids or budget only where ACOS is under 28% (stainless) or 18% (dual).
