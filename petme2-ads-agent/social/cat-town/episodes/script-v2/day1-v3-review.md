# Day 1 v3 script: review and rewrite (judged for virality first)

**Answer to "is this the best script?": No.**
- The draft adds the comment rule correctly, but it is about 72 words. At 2.5 words/s that is about 29 s, well over the 18-22 s target.
- It has no joke in the first 3 s, and it ends on two asks.
- The rewrite below keeps the owner's brief and his exact end line. It reuses only footage we already have, borrows Mayor Mango's best joke, and lands at about 54 words (about 20 s).

## Why it is more viral

- **Hook:** a joke lands by 2.9 s ("One vote. His own."). It is the kind of line people quote in comments and send to friends ("this is me"). Text is on frame 0, so the hook works muted and the cover makes sense.
- **Comment bait:**
  - The callback "He voted yes" invites replies like "Mango would vote for a nap tower".
  - The owner's own CTA is the only spoken ask, and it ends the video, so it is the last thing heard before the loop.
  - "Most likes in 24 h wins" adds a clock.
  - Pinned comment: "Most likes in 24 h gets built, with your @ on it. Weirder = better. Mango already voted for a tuna fountain (his vote doesn't count)."
- **Rewatch and loop:**
  - The 1,000-cat goal town (dense, funny signs) is the pause-and-rewatch frame.
  - The ending loops into the opening joke, so the second watch starts with the punchline.
- **Honesty and safety:**
  - The "mayor/vote" wording is a cute civic joke; never use real politics.
  - All demo content is stamped EXAMPLE.
  - No product line ("water and food" is gone); the feeder appears only as a 1.3 s joke backdrop.
