# PETME2 Cat Town: launch script options (v2, script only)

Owner idea: Mango is alone in town, no friends. Each follower brings a house + a cat. Let's build a cat town. The top-liked comment gets built.
Goal: funny, grabs attention in the first second, gets comments + follows. About 18-20 s, short simple English, deadpan narrator.
Shots must be doable in our 3D town (Mango, empty streets, fountain + feeder, house pops, building pops with confetti, 1,000-cat goal town).

---

## Option A: "Mayor Mango" (recommended)
| # | Voice | On screen | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "This is Mango. He just won the election for mayor." | MAYOR MANGO 🏆 | Mango in the square, confetti falls |
| 2 | "He got one vote." | VOTES: 1 | Hold on Mango, confetti stops |
| 3 | "His own." | (his own) | Slow push-in on Mango |
| 4 | "Because nobody else lives here." | POPULATION: 1 | Fast pull-out to the huge empty town |
| 5 | "No neighbors. No friends. Just a very big water bowl." | FRIENDS: 0 | Mango drinks alone at the giant fountain |
| 6 | "So let's fix this. Every follow brings a new cat..." | 1 FOLLOW = 1 CAT 🐱 | A house pops up, a cat appears |
| 7 | "...and a house with your name on it." | @YOU (example) | Name tag on the house |
| 8 | "And the top-liked comment? We build it. Anything." | TOP COMMENT = WE BUILD IT | Example building pops with confetti |
| 9 | "Cat airport? Sushi bar? Giant Mango statue? Sure." | (3 quick pops) | Three fast pops, marked example |
| 10 | "Let's build a cat town together." | LET'S BUILD A CAT TOWN | The 1,000-cat goal town |
| 11 | "Mayor Mango is waiting. Don't let him lose the next election." | FOLLOW + COMMENT 👇 | Back to lonely Mango, loops to line 1 |

Why it works: the "one vote… his own" joke lands in under 3 s; the word "mayor" makes a running character for daily videos ("Mayor Mango approves the cat airport").

## Option B: "Real estate ad"
1. "Now selling in Cat Town!" (SALE sign, happy music)
2. "Free water. Free food. Ocean view." (fountain, feeder, coast)
3. "Neighbors?" (music stops) ... "Mango." (Mango alone) "Just Mango."
4. "Price of a house: one follow. Every follow brings a new cat and a home with your name."
5. "And the top-liked comment decides what we build next."
6. "Cat airport. Sushi bar. Whatever you want."
7. "Let's build a cat town. Mango is lonely. Please hurry."

## Option C: "Mango's diary"
1. "Day 1 in Cat Town. Still the only cat." (Mango alone)
2. "I talked to the fountain today. It just splashed me." (fountain)
3. "I said hi to the food bowl. Rude. No answer." (feeder)
4. "Human says: every follow brings a new cat and a house."
5. "And the top comment gets built. Anything."
6. "Please. I need a friend. Or a cat airport."
7. "Let's build a cat town." (goal town) "Follow. Comment. Save me." (Mango)

Note: C needs Mango's voice (a second, different TTS voice) and works best once Mango has a face.
