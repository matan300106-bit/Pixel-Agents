# Cat Town - Day 1 (v2) Viral Blueprint
Format: 1080x1920, 30 fps, target 19 s (hard range 15-22 s). Engine render, TTS voice, code synth + pop SFX.

## 1. Why it can go viral
- **Lonely cat hook**: one tiny orange cat alone in a whole empty town. Instant empathy, readable muted, in under 1.5 s.
- **Your action = visible result**: following is not "support the brand", it is "a cat moves in with YOUR name on its house". The follow is the content.
- **Visible progress / series reason**: the counter "1 cat - Day 1" plus the 1,000-cat goal flash creates a gap viewers want to watch close. Follow = see the town grow (and maybe see your own house).
- **Comment bait**: "top comment builds anything" gives an easy, funny thing to type (cat airport, sushi bar, laser tower). Comments + replies feed ranking.
- **Rewatch loop**: last frame = first frame (Mango alone), so the loop restarts cleanly and boosts watch time.
- Brand stays invisible: the fountain and feeder are simply "the town's water and food". No product names, no prices, no links in the video.

**Critique of the old script**: (1) opens with "I'm building..." = creator-centric, slow, no image of the cat in the first second; (2) stacks two rules + two CTAs into 20 s, which dilutes the follow ask; (3) "day one: a fresh water fountain" is now false (fountain + feeder already exist) and made the product the payoff, which reads like an ad; (4) no stakes, no goal, no loop. Fix: lead with Mango's loneliness, ONE spoken rule (follow = cat), show the goal, put the comment rule on screen + pinned comment, loop back to Mango.

## 2. Hook options (first 1.5 s)
| # | Spoken (starts at 0.2 s) | On-screen text | First shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | "This is Mango. He's the only cat in this town." | "Population: 1 cat" | Extreme close-up on Mango's face, slow push OUT |
| B | "This town will have 1,000 cats. Right now..." | "Goal: 1,000 cats" | 1,000-cat town hero shot 1.2 s, hard cut to empty town |
| C | "Every follow moves one cat into this town." | "1 follow = 1 cat" | Top-down drone over empty lots, one "Reserved" sign |

**Pick: A.** Emotion first, works fully muted ("Population: 1 cat" over a lonely cat), and it sets up the loop ending. B's 1,000-cat flash is reused mid-video as the payoff; C's rule comes at beat 4 once we care about Mango.

## 6. Ending / loop + CTA
- Final frame (19.2 s) = extreme close on Mango in the square, framed identically to 0.0 s, music resolves on the same note the intro starts on, no fade to black.
- Loop line: viewer hears "...first neighbor." then "This is Mango." - reads as one sentence.
- Spoken CTA: "Follow, and be Mango's first neighbor." On-screen secondary CTA: "Top comment builds next". No end card with logos (end card = just the text over the Mango close-up).

## 8. Reviewer checklist (pass/fail)
1. Mango is visible and identifiable in frame 1 (0.0 s); first spoken word starts by 0.3 s.
2. Muted test: the first 1.5 s text alone ("Population: 1 cat") explains the premise.
3. Total length 15-22 s; no line longer than ~10 words.
4. The rule "1 follow = 1 new cat" is both spoken and shown on screen.
5. Visual change (cut, camera move, pop, or text change) at least every 3 s; no static stretch over 3 s.
6. 1,000-cat goal flash appears and is clearly different from the empty town.
7. Demo houses/name tags are marked as examples; counter never shows more than 1 cat.
8. Fountain and feeder appear naturally (Mango drinks and eats); no product name, price, logo or sales line anywhere.
9. All text inside safe zone (none above y 220, below y 1500, or right of x 940); max ~6 words on screen at once.
10. Subtitles sync word-by-word with current word yellow, and never overlap the big center text.
11. Last frame matches first frame framing; loop plays without a visible jump or black frame.
12. One spoken CTA (follow); comment CTA only as small text + caption + pinned comment.
13. Audio: voice clear over music (music ducked under voice), pop SFX on every counter/house pop, no clipping.
14. Exported 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, no letterboxing, no watermark.
15. Caption first line under 125 chars, 3-5 hashtags, cover with "Population: 1 cat" ready.
