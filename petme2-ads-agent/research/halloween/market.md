### 1.3 Patterns the winners share

1. **One white MAIN, then 7–8 warm lifestyle slots.** About 85–90% of secondary slots are lifestyle scenes with a pet and a headline. Pure-infographic slots on white are rare (dimensions and package only). The whole category uses the same **beige / tan / warm-wood palette** (dominant colours #c9b48e, #ccb79f, #d9c9af).
2. **Short headline plus 2–3 icons.** The leader, PETLIBRO, uses **0–12 words per slot**. Value brands go to 25–45 words. Ours averages ~25 words with generic headlines.
3. **Night-vision slot early (slot 2–3), shown as a dark image.** It appears in 7 of 10 stacks. This is the category's "spec flex".
4. **Real app-screen proof** (feeding log, schedule, notifications, live view) in 2–3 slots. This shows buyers the app works, which is their #1 fear.
5. **Reliability slots:** dual power with battery type spelled out (D cells), "keeps running offline", anti-clog, food-blocked and offline alerts.
6. **Privacy / camera-off slot** (MUBBI) and **"Package Includes"** (Faroro, faroro 3MP) appear in fast-rising new listings. Both are cheap trust builders.
7. **Emotional "away from home" framing:** "Go Out of Town Without Worry", "Even Miles Away", "Feel at ease, even when apart". Travel is the core use case.
8. **Video present on the leaders.** oneisall runs 4 videos.
9. **No competitor uses Halloween or seasonal imagery.** None of the 10 stacks has a pumpkin, black-cat or fall theme. The category is a sea of beige, so a tasteful **black-and-orange** palette would stand out in search and on the page.

### 3.2 How pet brands use seasonal creative on Amazon

- **Listing images:** none of the 10 top camera-feeder stacks uses seasonal art today (checked 2026-10-07). The norm across Amazon is that the MAIN stays white, and seasonal context shows up only in **secondary lifestyle images**, A+, Store and Sponsored Brands. Agencies advise **minor** listing-image updates for seasons (gifting cues) and warn against promo badges, which risk suppression ([Front Row](https://www.frontrowgroup.com/en/insights/the-amazon-q4-creative-playbook); [My Amazon Guy](https://myamazonguy.com/fba-holiday-readiness/amazon-holiday-creative-strategy/)).
- **Brand Store:** seasonal hero and a dedicated seasonal or deals page linked from the nav. Amazon's own Store prep guide says:
  - use seasonal imagery but "don't overdo" it;
  - remove outdated content after the event;
  - don't use Amazon event branding or numeric discount claims in Store creative;
  - header and tile product shots during an event must match ASINs with an active promotion.

  Source: [Amazon Ads key-events Store prep](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/key-events-store-prep).
- **Sponsored Brands:** seasonal lifestyle custom image or video landing on the Store. SB "drive page visit" ads that land on a Store showed **+28% ROAS**, and SB video landing on a Store showed **+23% conversion rate** vs landing on a product page. Recommended video length is about 20 s, within a 6–45 s range (same Amazon guide).

**Brand-safety rules for the shoot or AI generation:**
- No candy, chocolate or xylitol near pets. Shoppers will flag it as unsafe.
- No lit candles near the cat. Use LED lights only.
- No distressed animals in costumes. Cat costumes should be minimal (a small bandana at most), or skip them.
- No gore or horror faces.
- No trademarked characters (the "Mario" costume trend in the NRF data is off-limits).
- Props sit in the background, with "Decor not included" in small text if any prop sits next to the product.
- Night mood is risky because **we do not have verified night vision**. Any "Halloween night" scene must:
  1. show the live view on the phone as a **well-lit** room (lamps on, string lights), and
  2. never show a greenish or IR night image, the words "night", "dark", "infrared", or a dark room where the feeder is "watching".

  Use "evening" lighting with lamps on. This is the single biggest compliance trap in this brief, because 8 of 10 competitors use dark night-vision slots and the visual habit is easy to copy by mistake.

**Swap operations:**
- Produce all 3 versions (Halloween, holiday, evergreen) now.
- Upload the holiday set the evening of **Oct 31** (US time), because image updates can take hours to propagate.
- Save the previous files under dated names so they can be reverted.
- Never edit MAIN for the season.
- After Oct 31, also update the video and any SB creative the same day. Stale Halloween ads in November look unattended.

## 4. Amazon image rules (2026), as they apply to this listing

### 4.1 MAIN image

- **Background:** pure white RGB 255,255,255.
- **Fill:** the product fills **≥85%** of the frame.
- **Nothing extra:** no text, logos, watermarks, badges, borders, insets, colour blocks, props or items not included.
- **No phone or app mockups inside the MAIN.** MUBBI and Yuposl do this, and it is a grey zone that can get the listing suppressed. Don't copy it.
- **No mannequins**, and no pets in the MAIN for this category.
- **Real product only.** It must be a photo or faithful render of exactly what ships (white unit, stainless bowl).
- **Zoom:** at least 1,000 px on the longest side enables zoom. We use **2000 × 2000 px**.
- **File format:** JPEG preferred (PNG, TIFF and non-animated GIF also accepted), **sRGB**, under 10 MB, max 10,000 px.

Sources: [BeBold Digital 2026](https://www.bebolddigital.com/blog/amazon-image-requirements), [Soona 2026 specs](https://soona.co/image-resizer/amazon-image-size-specs), [SellerSprite 2026](https://www.sellersprite.com/en/blog/Amazon-Image-Requirements-Optimization-Tips).

### 4.2 Secondary images: allowed, but no:

- **Amazon marks:**
  - Amazon logos or trademarks, or anything confusingly similar ("Amazon", "Prime", "Alexa", the smile).
  - Badge replicas: "Amazon's Choice", "Best Seller", "Top Seller", "Overall Pick".
  - **"Prime Big Deal"** or any Amazon event name.
- **Social proof we don't have:** star ratings, review counts, testimonials or quotes, "#1", "best", "top rated".
- **Promotional text:** prices, "% off", "sale", "deal", "limited time", countdowns, "free shipping".
- **Competitor names** or side-by-side comparisons with other brands. Comparing our own products is fine.
- **Unverified specs:** night vision, 2K, 5 GHz, motion alerts, cloud recording, offline scheduling, kibble size or meals per day until each is verified (`images-aplus-plan.md` §3.3).
- **Health or medical claims** for pets ("reduces anxiety", "prevents obesity", "vet recommended"). Use behaviour language instead, e.g. "Talk to them while you're away".
- **Guarantees or warranties** unless the exact term is offered on Amazon. Keep the "30-day refund" from the Shopify playbook off Amazon images.
- **Implied accessories:** props that imply they are included. Add "Decor not included" if a prop is close to the product.
- **AI-generated people:** follow Amazon's AI-content labelling requirement.

Sources: [eStore Factory](https://www.estorefactory.com/blog/amazon-image-requirements-best-practices-101/), [Ecombrainly](https://ecombrainly.com/amazon-image-requirements/), [SellerLabs AI images](https://www.sellerlabs.com/knowledge-base/generating-product-images-and-lifestyle-shots-with-ai-whats-allowed/).

**Practical specs:**
- 2000 × 2000 px, sRGB JPEG, under 5 MB.
- Mobile-legible text: headline 6 words or fewer, 18 pt+ equivalent at 375 px width, 3 callouts or fewer.
- Keep seasonal text off the outer 5% edge, where mobile crops it.

### 4.3 A+ / Brand Story / Store / Sponsored Brands

- **A+:** no prices, promos, time-limited language, guarantees, competitor comparisons or seller links. A seasonal A+ banner is possible, but A+ review can take up to 7 days. Starting now risks it going live late and outdated on Nov 1. **Skip seasonal A+** and put the effort into the Store and SB instead.
- **Brand Store:** seasonal imagery is welcome, but:
  - no Amazon event branding;
  - no numeric discount claims in creative;
