## 5. Problems found

**Keyword vs product**
- "wifi cat feeder" (Feeders - Exact): OK for QO (title says WiFi). LW and 93 say "App Control" only (app = WiFi, so OK, but the title does not say WiFi -> lower CTR for those two). KEEP.
- "2.2l cat water fountain": size correct for SD and JE. But top results are stainless; ours are plastic. KEEP at low bid, watch CTR.
- Do **not** use "stainless steel" keywords for the 2.2L fountains. JE has only a stainless **tray**.
- Supplement: no fish oil -> do **not** add "salmon oil for cats" / "fish oil for cats".
- Camera feeder: no night vision in listing -> do **not** add "night vision" keywords.
- Filters fit **PETME2 fountains only**. Generic filter keywords will bring Veken/Catit/PetSafe owners who cannot use them -> low sales.

**Products that cannot serve (All Products file)**
- Filters (5 SKUs), Grooming (3), Supplement (1), Feeder+Waterer (2): **all 0 stock, no price**. 4 ASINs not found in US catalog. ROJECO and FIXR are not PETME2 brand.

**Duplicates / overlap**
- "automatic cat feeder" exact (Feeders-Exact) + broad (Feeders-Broad): normal exact/broad pair, but broad group needs negative exact so exact wins.
- Feeders-Broad "automatic cat feeder" (broad) will also catch "automatic cat feeder with camera" -> competes with Camera Feeder - Exact.
- "cat water fountain" + "pet water fountain" exact (Fountains-Exact) and broad (Fountains-Broad): same as above.
- Fountains-Broad "cat water fountain" (broad) also catches "stainless steel cat water fountain" (Stainless-Exact) and "automatic cat water fountain" / "2.2l cat water fountain" (All Products).
- No exact duplicates across campaigns with the same match type.

**Own negatives blocking own keywords**
- Feeders negative "water" (phrase): does NOT block "automatic cat feeder and water dispenser" - that keyword is in the All Products campaign (negatives are per campaign). OK. It correctly stops Feeders-Broad from showing on water-dispenser searches.
- Fountains negative "filter" (phrase): blocks Fountains-Broad from "cat water fountain with filter". OK - All Products has it as exact. But plural "filters" (e.g. "cat fountain filters") may not be blocked -> add "filters".
- Fountains negative "feeder": no conflict.
- All Products campaign has **no negatives at all**. OK for exact-only groups.

**Match types**
- All exact groups are exact. Broad only in the 2 broad (discovery) groups. Good.

**Too broad / waste**
- "pet feeder" broad: 135k results, gravity feeders, bowls, dog feeders. Pause.
- "pet grooming kit": clippers/scissors. Remove (group on hold anyway).
- "cat skin and coat": lickable treats. Remove (group on hold anyway).
- "pet feeder and waterer": gravity sets. Remove (group on hold anyway).
