Quick summary

  • Dry food feeders (like PETME2) use a tank and a turning mechanism. Wet food would jam them and spoil. Do not put wet food in them.
  • Wet food feeders are usually rotating trays or timed lids, sometimes with an ice pack.
  • Food safety is the big limit. Wet food should not sit at room temperature for long. A common guideline is no more than about 2 to 4 hours, and less in warm rooms.
  • Ice packs help a little, but they only keep food cool for a limited time.
  • A mixed plan works well for many cats: dry meals from an automatic feeder, wet meals by hand when you are home.

Can you use an automatic cat feeder for wet food?

Many cat owners want an automatic cat feeder for wet food. It makes sense. Wet food is popular, many cats love it, and it adds water to the diet. But wet food is much harder to automate than kibble, for two reasons:

  1. It does not flow. Dry kibble pours from a tank and can be measured by a turning wheel or screw. Wet food is sticky and soft, so it cannot be stored in a tank and dispensed the same way.
  2. It spoils. Once a can or pouch is open, wet food is a fresh, moist food. Left at room temperature, it can grow bacteria and go bad. Dry food is made to stay shelf-stable for much longer.

So wet food feeders do not dispense food the way dry feeders do. Instead, you portion the food ahead of time into small trays, and the feeder opens or turns to give your cat access to one tray at a time.

What types of automatic feeders work with wet food?

There are three main types: rotating tray feeders, timed lid feeders, and feeders with ice packs or cooling.

Here are the main types you will see when you shop. We are describing the general designs, not specific brands.

1. Rotating tray feeders

A round base with several small compartments under a lid. The lid has one opening. At each set time, the tray turns so the next compartment lines up with the opening. You fill each compartment in advance.

  • Good: can hold several small meals; simple to use.
  • Watch out for: food in later compartments sits for hours before it is served. Without cooling, that is not safe for wet food. Some clever cats learn to turn the tray by hand.

2. Timed lid feeders

One or two bowls with lids that pop open at a set time. These are often used to give one meal later in the day, such as lunch while you are at work.

  • Good: easy to clean; good for one delayed meal.
  • Watch out for: usually only one or two meals; food still sits until the lid opens.

3. Feeders with ice packs or cooling

Some tray and lid feeders have a slot under the bowls for a frozen ice pack. A few higher-end models have powered cooling. The idea is to keep the wet food cool until it is served.

  • Good: helps keep food cooler for a delayed meal.
  • Watch out for: a basic ice pack only works for a limited time, often a few hours depending on the room. It is not the same as a fridge. Warm rooms and sunny spots make it melt faster.

Wet food safety: how long can wet cat food sit out?

This is the most important part of choosing an automatic cat feeder for wet food. Here are common general guidelines. Always check the label on your cat's food, since brands give their own advice.

Situation Common general guideline
Wet food in a bowl at room temperature Remove uneaten food after about 2 to 4 hours. In a hot room, sooner.
Wet food in a feeder with a frozen ice pack May stay cool a bit longer, but plan for a short delay, not a full day. Follow the feeder maker's limits.
Opened can or pouch, covered, in the fridge Often about 3 to 5 days, or as the label says.
Food that smells off, looks dry, or has been out in the heat Throw it away.

A few more safety tips:

  • Wash trays and bowls after every use with hot, soapy water. Old food left in corners can grow bacteria.
  • Keep the feeder out of the sun and away from heaters.
  • Do not top up old food with new food. Clean the bowl first.
  • Never leave wet food in a feeder for a weekend trip. For trips longer than a short day, ask a pet sitter or friend to serve wet meals.

What works (and what doesn't) for wet food?

A cooled feeder for one delayed wet meal can work. Leaving wet food out for many hours or days does not.

How do you feed wet and dry food with an automatic feeder?

Put dry food in the automatic feeder for scheduled meals, and serve wet food by hand when you are home.

Many cats do well on a mix of wet and dry food. If your vet agrees that a mix is fine for your cat, this simple plan gives you the best of both:

  1. Wet meals by hand when you are home, for example breakfast at 7 a.m. and dinner at 6 p.m. Serve, wait about 20 to 30 minutes, then pick up and wash the bowl.
  2. Dry meals from an automatic feeder in between, for example a small meal at noon, one at 10 p.m., and one at 4 a.m.
  3. Keep the daily total the same. Split your cat's daily calories between wet and dry. Do not add a full wet diet on top of a full dry diet.
  4. Add a fountain nearby, since dry meals bring very little water.

Here is how that could look for a typical day:

Time Meal Who serves it

7 a.m. Wet meal You
12 p.m. Small dry meal Automatic feeder
6 p.m. Wet meal You

This keeps wet food fresh, gives your cat several small meals, and means nobody has to get up at 4 a.m. To work out the right daily amount, see our cat feeding chart by weight.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an automatic cat feeder for wet food?

Yes. Most are rotating tray feeders or timed lid feeders, and some have ice packs for cooling. They work best for one short delayed meal, not for leaving wet food out all day.

Can I put wet food in a regular automatic feeder?

No. Feeders with a food tank, including all PETME2 feeders, are made for dry kibble only. Wet food can jam the mechanism and spoil inside the feeder.

How long can wet cat food sit out?

A common general guideline is about 2 to 4 hours at room temperature, and less in a warm room. Check your food's label, and throw out food that has been sitting longer.

Do ice packs keep wet food safe in a feeder?

Ice packs help keep food cooler for a while, but only for a limited time. Think of them as a way to delay one meal by a few hours, not as a fridge.

Can I leave wet food in an automatic feeder overnight?

It is not a good idea for most feeders, because the food may sit at an unsafe temperature for many hours. A dry food feeder is a safer choice for overnight and early morning meals.

What is the best way to feed wet food when I am at work?

Serve wet meals before you leave and when you get home. For meals in between, use a dry food automatic feeder. If your cat must eat only wet food, a cooled timed feeder for one lunch meal, or a pet sitter, can help.

Is wet cat food better than dry?

Neither is better for every cat. Wet food adds a lot of water to the diet and many cats love it, while dry food is easier to store, measure and use in an automatic feeder. Many cats do well on either or a mix, as long as the food is complete and balanced, so ask your vet what suits your cat.

Should I feed my cat wet or dry food?

Either can work, and many owners feed a mix. Wet food helps with water intake, and dry food is convenient for scheduled meals in an automatic feeder. Ask your vet, especially if your cat has a health condition or needs to lose weight.

Is dry cat food bad for cats?

No. A complete and balanced dry food is fine for most healthy cats. Cats on dry food get little water from their meals, so keep fresh water available at all times. Your vet may suggest more wet food for some health conditions.

Can I mix wet and dry food for my cat?

Many cats do well on a mix. Keep the total daily calories the same by splitting them between wet and dry, and ask your vet what balance is right for your cat.

How much wet and dry food should I feed my cat?

Split your cat's daily calories between wet and dry so the total stays the same. For example, if a cat needs about 240 calories a day and eats one 3 oz can of about 80 calories, the dry food should supply about 160 calories, a little over 1/3 cup of a kibble with about 400 calories per cup. Check your labels and ask your vet.

Which automatic pet feeder is most reliable?

Dry food feeders are generally the most dependable type, because kibble portions evenly while wet food can jam or spoil. Look for an anti-jam design, backup power so meals continue during a power cut, and an app or display that confirms each meal. Test any new feeder for a few days while you are home.

What is the best automatic cat feeder?

The best automatic cat feeder is the one that fits your cat's food, the number of cats, and your routine. For dry food, look for reliable portion control, easy scheduling, backup power, and parts you can clean. For wet food, a timed tray feeder with cooling suits one delayed meal only.

What should I use for a weekend away?

A dry food automatic feeder plus a water fountain can cover meals and water for a short trip, but someone should still check on your cat. If your cat eats wet food, ask a pet sitter to serve those meals.
