What does a cat water fountain filter do?

A fountain recirculates the same water over and over. The filter sits in the water path and catches things you do not want floating around, such as cat hair, dust, and small bits of food. Many filters also contain activated carbon, which is commonly used to reduce odors and improve how water tastes, and ion-exchange resin, which is often included to reduce some minerals from tap water.

Different fountains use different filter styles. Here are the most common:

Foam or sponge pre-filters

These wrap around or sit in front of the pump. Their main job is to catch hair before it reaches the pump. They are usually rinsed often and replaced less often.

Cartridge filters

A cartridge is a small pouch or frame filled with carbon and resin beads, often wrapped in mesh. The PETME2 Transparent 2.2L fountain uses a filter cartridge.

Multi-layer filters

These stack several materials together, such as a fine mesh, a cotton or foam layer, activated carbon, and resin. Both the PETME2 3.2L stainless steel fountain and the 2.2L fountain with stainless steel tray use a 4-layer filter.

Always check your fountain's manual. If it gives a different schedule, follow the manual.

Signs it is time for a new filter

  • Color change. A white or light filter that has turned gray, brown, or green is done.
  • Slower flow. A clogged filter can reduce how much water reaches the spout.
  • Louder pump. When water struggles to get through, the pump can work harder and sound noisier.
  • Smell. A musty or sour smell means it is time to replace the filter and clean the fountain.
  • Visible slime or hair stuck in the filter that will not rinse out.

How to change the filter, step by step

  1. Unplug the fountain.
  2. Remove the cover and lift out the old filter.
  3. Clean the fountain while it is apart. This is the perfect time for the weekly clean (see our guide to cleaning a cat water fountain).
  4. Prepare the new filter. Many carbon filters should be rinsed or soaked in clean water for a few minutes before use. This removes loose carbon dust and helps them sit properly. Follow the instructions on the package.
  5. Put the new filter in place. Make sure it faces the right way and fits snugly so water goes through it, not around it.
  6. Refill and plug in. Check that water flows normally. A few small bubbles at first are normal.

Can you reuse or wash a filter?

You can (and should) rinse a filter with clean water during weekly cleaning. Do not use soap, because it can stay in the filter material and affect the water. But rinsing does not "recharge" carbon or resin. Once those materials are used up, they stop working well, even if the filter looks okay. That is why filters have a replacement window.

Foam pre-filters are a bit different. Many can be rinsed and reused for longer. Check your manual to see which parts are reusable.

Tips to make filters last longer

  • Rinse weekly. Removing hair early keeps the filter working through its full cycle.
  • Brush your cat, especially during shedding season.
  • Keep the fountain away from food and litter so less debris gets in.
  • Use filtered water if your tap water is very hard.
  • Write the change date on a piece of tape on the fountain base, or set a phone reminder.
  • Buy filters ahead. Keeping a spare at home means you never stretch an old filter too long.

Does a fountain with a better filter make a difference?

A good filter makes upkeep easier, but the bigger factors are how often you clean and how easy the fountain is to take apart. FAQ

What happens if I don't change my cat fountain filter?

An old filter can clog, slow the water flow, make the pump work harder, and stop catching hair and debris well. The water can also start to smell. Changing it on schedule keeps the fountain working as it should.

Can I run a cat fountain without a filter?

Most fountains will still pump water without a filter, but hair and debris will reach the pump more easily. It is best to use the filter the fountain was designed for.

Why is my new filter making the water cloudy?

New carbon filters can release a little fine dust. Rinsing or soaking the filter before use, as the package directs, usually prevents this.

Are all cat fountain filters the same?

No. Filters are usually made to fit specific fountain models. Always use a filter that matches your fountain's size and shape so water passes through it properly.
