The pump is the heart of any cat water fountain. It keeps water moving and pushes it through the filter. It is also the part most owners forget to clean. Over time, cat hair, food bits, and minerals collect inside, and the fountain gets louder, slower, or stops. This guide shows you how to clean a cat water fountain pump step by step, how often to change the water, and how to fix the most common fountain problems.

How does a cat fountain pump work?

A fountain pump is a small, sealed electric motor that sits under the water. Inside, a tiny spinning part called the impeller pulls water in through an intake and pushes it up to the spout.

Most pumps have three parts you can clean: the outer cover (sometimes with a small sponge or pre-filter), the impeller (a small rotor with fins, held in place by a magnet), and the housing around the impeller. Hair and slime love to wrap around the impeller. That is the number one reason a fountain gets noisy or slow.

How to clean a cat water fountain pump (step by step)

Cleaning the pump takes about 10 minutes. Do it as part of your regular fountain clean. Always check your fountain's manual first, because designs vary.

  1. Unplug the fountain. Never touch the pump or put your hands in the water while it is plugged in.
  2. Empty the fountain. Pour out the water and take off the top and the filter.
  3. Take out the pump. Gently pull it off the base. It is often held by suction cups. Do not pull it by the cord.
  4. Open the pump cover. Most covers pop or twist off. Set aside any sponge or pre-filter.
  5. Remove the impeller. Pull it straight out with your fingers. It is held by a magnet, so it slides out easily.
  6. Remove hair and gunk. Pick off any hair wrapped around the impeller shaft.
  7. Scrub every part. Use warm water, a drop of mild unscented soap, and a soft small brush or old toothbrush on the cover, impeller, and inside the housing. Use a pipe cleaner or cotton swab for the small water channel.
  8. Remove mineral build-up. If you see white crust (scale), soak the cover and impeller in a mix of about half white vinegar and half warm water for 15 to 30 minutes, then scrub again. Do not soak the motor part or the cord.
  9. Rinse very well. Rinse every part under clean running water until no soap or vinegar smell is left. Cats can be put off by these smells.
  10. Put it back together. Return the impeller, close the cover, and press the pump back into its spot.
  11. Refill and test. Fill to the line with fresh water, put the filter and top back on, dry your hands, and plug it in. Water should flow smoothly within a few seconds.

For the rest of the fountain (bowl, top, and spout), follow our full guide on how often to clean a cat water fountain.

How often should you clean a cat fountain pump?

Many owners clean the pump about every 2 to 4 weeks. Clean it sooner if you have several pets, a long-haired cat, hard water, or if the fountain gets louder or the flow drops.

Task How often (general guideline)
Top up with fresh waterEvery day or two
Change all the water and wash the bowlAbout once a week (every 3 to 5 days with several pets)
Rinse the filter (plain water)At each weekly clean
Clean the pump and impellerAbout every 2 to 4 weeks
Replace the filterAbout every 2 to 4 weeks (see your manual)

How often to change water in a cat fountain

Top up the water every day or two, and change all of it at least once a week when you clean the fountain. A fountain filters and moves the water, but it does not make old water new.

Change the water sooner if you see hair, food, or slime, or if your cat seems less interested in drinking. Always keep the water above the minimum line. A pump running low or dry can get loud and can be damaged.

How to clean a stainless steel cat fountain

Clean a stainless steel fountain with warm water, mild unscented soap, and a soft sponge or cloth. Avoid steel wool, scouring powder, and bleach, which can scratch or damage the finish.

  1. Unplug and empty the fountain. Remove the pump and filter.
  2. Wash the steel bowl and top with warm soapy water and a soft sponge.
  3. For white water spots or scale, wipe or soak with a mix of about half white vinegar and half water for 15 to 30 minutes.
  4. Rinse very well and dry with a soft cloth to prevent new spots.
  5. Many stainless steel bowls are dishwasher safe, but the pump and any plastic parts often are not. Check your manual.
  6. Clean the pump as described above, then reassemble and refill.

Why is my cat water fountain not working?

Most fountains that stop working have one of four simple problems: hair in the impeller, low water, an air lock, or a loose power connection. Check these before you think about replacing anything.

How to fix an air lock in a fountain pump

An air lock happens when a bubble of air gets trapped in the pump, so it spins but cannot move water. It is common after cleaning or when the water gets too low.

  1. Unplug the fountain.
  2. Make sure the water is above the minimum line.
  3. Take the pump out and hold it fully under the water. Gently squeeze or tilt it to let the bubbles escape.
  4. Put the pump back while it is still under water, then plug in.
  5. If needed, unplug and plug back in a few times to help push the air out.

Why is my cat fountain so loud?

A fountain usually gets loud because the water is low, the impeller is dirty, or the pump is vibrating against the bowl. Topping up and cleaning the pump fixes most cases. Some cats will avoid a noisy fountain, so it is worth fixing quickly.

When to replace the pump or the fountain

Replace the pump if it still does not run, or stays loud, after a full clean and an air lock check. Also replace it right away if you see any damage.

  • The cord, plug, or housing is cracked, cut, or chewed. Stop using it at once.
  • The impeller is cracked, worn, or wobbles even when clean.
  • The motor hums but the impeller does not spin, even after cleaning.
  • The pump gets hot to the touch.
  • The flow stays weak with a new filter and a clean pump.

Some fountains sell replacement pumps. With others, it can make sense to replace the whole fountain, especially if the bowl is scratched plastic that is hard to keep clean.

Tips to keep your fountain pump running longer

  • Never let it run dry. Keep water above the minimum line.
  • Clean on a schedule. Set a phone reminder for the weekly clean and the pump clean.
  • Brush your cat. Less loose hair means less hair in the pump.
  • Use filtered water if you have hard water. It can mean less mineral build-up.
  • Keep food away. Place the fountain a few feet from the food bowl so crumbs do not fall in.
  • Rinse well after cleaning. Leftover soap can foam and make cats avoid the water.

Frequently asked questions

How do you clean a cat water fountain pump?

Unplug the fountain, remove the pump, and open its cover. Take out the impeller, remove any hair, and scrub all parts with warm water, mild unscented soap, and a small brush. Rinse very well, reassemble, and refill with fresh water.

How often should I clean my cat fountain pump?

Many owners clean the pump about every 2 to 4 weeks. Clean it sooner with several pets, long-haired cats, or hard water. A good habit is to clean the pump every time you replace the filter.

How often should you change the water in a cat fountain?

Top up the water every day or two, and change all of it at least once a week during a full clean. Change it sooner if you see hair, food, or slime. Never let the water drop below the minimum line.

Why is my cat water fountain not working?

The most common causes are hair in the impeller, low water, an air lock, or a loose power connection. Top up the water, clean the pump, and check the plug first. If it still does not run after that, the pump may need replacing.

Why is my cat fountain making a loud noise?

A loud fountain usually has low water, a dirty impeller, or a pump vibrating against the bowl. Top it up, clean the impeller, and make sure the pump sits flat on its suction cups. Most noise problems go away after a clean.

How do I fix an air lock in my fountain pump?

Unplug the fountain and make sure the water is above the minimum line. Hold the pump fully under the water and tilt or squeeze it gently to release bubbles. Put it back while it is still submerged and plug it in again.

Can I use vinegar to clean a cat water fountain?

Yes, a mix of about half white vinegar and half water helps remove white mineral scale. Soak parts for 15 to 30 minutes, then scrub. Rinse very well afterward so no vinegar smell is left, and do not soak the pump's motor or cord.

How do you clean a stainless steel cat fountain?

Wash the steel parts with warm water, mild unscented soap, and a soft sponge. Use a vinegar and water soak for water spots, then rinse and dry. Avoid steel wool, scouring powder, and bleach, which can damage the finish.

Can I put my cat water fountain in the dishwasher?

Some fountain bowls and tops are dishwasher safe, but the pump and electrical parts never are. Check your manual for each part. When in doubt, hand wash with warm soapy water.

Can a cat fountain pump run dry?

A pump should not run dry. Running with low or no water can make it loud, cause overheating, and shorten its life. Keep water above the minimum line, and use a fountain with a low-water light if you tend to forget.

How long does a cat fountain pump last?

Pump life varies a lot by model, water type, and care. Regular cleaning and never letting it run dry help it last longer. Replace it if it will not run, stays loud after cleaning, or shows any damage.

Why is there pink slime in my cat fountain?

Pink or orange slime is usually a biofilm caused by bacteria that grow in damp places. It means the fountain needs a full clean of every part, including the pump. Cleaning more often helps stop it coming back.

What is the impeller in a cat fountain pump?

The impeller is the small spinning part with fins inside the pump that moves the water. It is held in place by a magnet and pulls out easily for cleaning. Hair wrapped around it is the most common cause of noise and slow flow.

When should I replace my cat fountain filter?

Most fountain filters should be replaced about every 2 to 4 weeks, or as your manual says. Replace it sooner if it looks dark or clogged or the flow slows. Rinse it with plain water during your weekly clean.
