# Competitor listing study for the PETME2 SEO rewrite (2026-10-04)

Source: SP-API catalog search (summaries, salesRanks) plus item detail (attributes) and competitivePrice for 38 competitor ASINs. Also Helium 10 Cerebro CSVs. Read only. Full data is in `competitors-listings.json`.
Rank = category best seller rank, shared by all variations of a parent ASIN. Lower means more sales.
Use competitor text only to learn their structure. Do not copy it.

## Patterns across all groups

- **Exact search phrase first.** The top sellers put the head keyword in the first 40 characters, for example "Automatic Cat Feeder for 2 Cats", "Automatic Cat Feeder with Camera" or "Cat Water Fountain Stainless Steel:".
- **Titles are either short or full length.** Several top-10 listings use a short title of 64 to 75 characters (Veken #1, oneisall #3, Neareal #3). The others use 170 to 200 characters.
- **Capacity in two units.** Feeders write "5L/20 Cups". Fountains write "108oz/3.2L".
- **Bullet format.** Each bullet has a short header and then 250 to 450 characters. The format is "Header: text", 【Header】, or ALL CAPS. Every top listing gives hard numbers: grams per portion, meals per day, kibble size, days of food, dB, filter stages, bowl spacing in inches and battery type.
- **Warranty.** Some competitors state it: Frienhund and Petlipo offer 1 to 2 years, and PETLIBRO and PETKIT offer 2 years.

## Dual 3L feeders (B0GHLSQMJ9, B0GHMCG8Q9), $49.99

- **#1:** oneisall B0C5X2G933 (#3 in Automatic Cat Feeders, $49.99). It has no WiFi. It wins with an exact-match title and concrete numbers.
- **Gap:** at the same price we offer app control and 2-way audio. Most competitors only have a 10-second voice recorder.
- **Title:** start with "PETME2 Automatic Cat Feeder for 2 Cats". B0GHMCG8Q9 currently starts with "Cat Treat Dispenser", which is weak.
- **Bullets:** cover 2-cat fairness, app, 2-way audio, capacity in cups and days of food, portion size and meals per day, freshness, battery type for dual power, and dishwasher-safe stainless bowls. Use numbers only if they are verified.

## 5L WiFi elevated dual feeder (B0GHH8L59K), $45.99

- **Top sellers:** PETLIBRO B09S8WMJY9 (#2) and ANDOLL (#1, $31.99, no WiFi).
- **Gap:** ours is the cheapest 5L WiFi dual-bowl feeder found. Competitors charge $49.99 to $94.99. Only MUBBI also sells an elevated design.
- **Title:** use "Automatic Cat Feeder for 2 Cats, 5L WiFi…" and add "Elevated". Add the WiFi band only if we have checked it.

## Stainless 3.2L fountain (B0DR7FCLZR), $39.99

- **#1:** Veken B0FPFZ3C5K (#1 in Cat Fountains, $27.99, 108oz). Neareal B0C8MHHVPC ($33.24) is the same size as ours.
- **Title problem:** our title starts with "Luxury Smart Pet Fountain 2026". It has no brand and no keyword near the front.
- **Title:** use "PETME2 Stainless Steel Cat Water Fountain, 108oz/3.2L…".
- **Bullets:** lead with what we have that competitors rarely combine: dual flow modes, LED, 4-layer filter and dishwasher safe. Add a dB value only if it has been measured.

## $19.99 plastic fountains (B0GHKN9DBR, B0GHLBGCP3, B0GHKRYV6W)

- **Head term:** stainless fountains own "cat water fountain". Our plastic fountains rank 269 to 293 for it. Veken C1 is $13.99.
- **Long-tail terms:** target terms where competitors rank 1 to 15:
  - "water fountains for cats indoor" (49.5k)
  - "cat water bowl" (27k)
  - "cat water dispenser" (17.5k)
  - "automatic water dispenser for cats" (8.8k)
- **Features to sell:** anti-dry pump stop, low-water light, visible water level and the 4-layer filter. PETKIT and Veken sell on the same features.
- **Title:** write capacity as "74oz/2.2L". On B0GHKRYV6W, put "Stainless Steel Tray" and the number of filters included in the title.
